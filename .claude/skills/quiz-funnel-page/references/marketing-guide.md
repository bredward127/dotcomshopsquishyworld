# Marketing guide deliverable

Template: `templates/docs/marketing-guide-template.html`. It's 8 Letter pages, one per section,
in the same palette. Render it with `scripts/render-pdf.mjs` (it inlines Google Fonts so the
PDF doesn't fall back to system fonts). Check the page count. If a section spills onto an
extra page, tighten spacing rather than cutting content.

Sections: cover · 01 funnel and profiles · 02 measurement (events, sub-tags, UTM
rules, weekly scorecard) · 03 benchmarks · 04 creative playbook (angles per persona,
quiz-first creative, weekly batch of ~5) · 05 channels (Meta, TikTok/Reels organic,
Pinterest, own content, careful Search) plus a starting budget split labeled as an
assumption · 06 weekly loop and page experiments · 07 compliance.

## Benchmark facts you may quote (Motion "Creative Benchmarks 2026", Meta ads,
Sept 2025–Jan 2026, 578,750 creatives, 6,015 advertisers, $1.29B spend)
- Winner = spend ≥10× the account median ad and ≥$500. About 5% of ads are winners;
  10× is about the 92.3rd percentile; expect at most about 1 in 10–13.
- Spend split: about 55% to winners, 28% to mid-range, 17% to losers. Share to winners rises by tier
  (Micro ~23% → Enterprise ~64%).
- Avg creatives/week, all vs top 25%: Micro 2.8 vs 4.8 · Small 4.1 vs 8.0 ·
  Medium 6.6 vs 15.9 · Large 11.2 vs 31.1 · Enterprise 18.8 vs 54.6.
  Avg hit rate: 4.0 / 6.4 / 8.1 / 8.6 / 8.8 %.
- Micro tier: 50.2% losers / 46.0% mid / 3.7% winners of creatives; 31.5 / 45.6 /
  23.0 % of spend. Mid-range ads carry small accounts, so don't kill steady spenders.
- Offer-first banners and demos are scale formats. Text-forward assets (text-only,
  image + text, simple GIFs) appear among winners more than expected. Unboxing, POV and BTS
  have high hit rates but low spend use. Clarity and immediacy hooks surface often; curiosity and
  confessional hooks stop the scroll.

Always phrase these as **associations**, measured by spend (not ROAS), from a
holiday-heavy window. Never invent target conversion rates for the funnel. Tell
the user to set baselines from their first ~500 quiz views.
