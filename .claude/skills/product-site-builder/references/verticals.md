# Vertical playbooks

Qualitative guidance for matching a page to a product category. The Creative
Benchmarks 2026 report confirms that top visual styles **differ by vertical** (a format
strong overall may barely appear in another category), but its per-vertical tables live in
an appendix that is not bundled. **Do not quote per-vertical numbers** unless the user
supplies that data. The cross-vertical figures in marketing-guide.md are fine to use.

| Vertical | Default archetype | Preset | Lead with | Trust elements (only if true) | Compliance watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health & Wellness | Quiz or landing | clinical or pastel | Routine fit, how it feels to use | Return policy, materials, third-party testing docs | No treat/cure/prevent claims; no health-attribute ad copy; no health-based targeting |
| Beauty & Personal Care | Quiz (skin/hair type) | clinical or natural | Before-and-after of routine (not results claims), texture close-ups | Ingredient list, dermatologist-tested only with docs | Results claims need substantiation; no "clinically proven" without a study |
| Food & Nutrition / Beverage | Landing or collection; quiz for flavor-profile catalogs | bold or natural | Taste, usage moments, the recipe | Ingredients, origin, allergen info | Supplements: FDA disclaimer, no disease claims; alcohol: age gate |
| Fashion & Apparel | Collection; quiz for fit/style | bold or natural | On-body imagery, fit guidance | Size guide, returns | Sustainability claims must be specific (FTC Green Guides) |
| Home & Lifestyle | Landing or comparison | natural | In-room context, dimensions, materials | Warranty, dimensions, care | Safety certifications only if documented |
| Technology / Gadgets | Comparison or landing | dark or clinical | Demo, specs that matter, compatibility | Spec table, warranty | Battery/safety claims; accurate specs |
| Pets | Quiz (breed/size/age) or landing | pastel or natural | The pet using it, durability | Materials, size guide | Pet-health claims are regulated like human ones in spirit: keep them modest |
| Parenting, Family & Kids | Quiz or comparison | pastel | Age fit, safety, mess level | Age grading, material safety docs | Children's privacy (COPPA): don't collect kids' data; no targeting minors |
| Fitness & Sports | Landing or quiz (level/goal) | bold or dark | Demo in use, progression | Returns, dimensions | No guaranteed results or weight-loss claims |
| Education | Lead-gen or waitlist | clinical | Outcome, curriculum, schedule | Real credentials, real schedule | No guaranteed job or grade outcomes |
| Finance | Lead-gen or comparison | clinical | Clear costs and terms | Licensing, regulator IDs | Heavily regulated: required disclosures; get legal review |
| Travel & Hospitality | Collection or lead-gen | natural | Place imagery, itinerary | Real policies, cancellation terms | Total-price display rules |
| Professional / Local Services | Lead-gen | clinical or natural | Service area, response time, process | License/insurance, real reviews with permission | Don't fake reviews or badges; local-ad rules for regulated trades |
| Automotive | Comparison or lead-gen | dark | Fitment, specs | Fitment table, warranty | Accurate fitment; no safety claims without basis |
| Entertainment & Media | Landing or waitlist | dark or bold | The experience itself, clips | Release info | Age ratings |
| Toys, fidgets, sensory | Quiz | pastel | Texture, noise, size, mess | Age grading, materials | No therapy/medical claims; see compliance.md |

## Ad angle starters by archetype
- **Quiz**: the question as hook ("Slow-rise, heavy, or gel? 4 taps"), result reveal, text-only static.
- **Landing**: demo in the first second, a concrete reason to act, POV of use.
- **Comparison**: "We compared N [products] so you don't have to"; a single-spec showdown.
- **Lead-gen**: the specific problem plus response time ("Leak? A roofer calls back today").
